word-break: keep-all breaks after commas and full stops inside numbers and abbreviations, and after an opening parenthesis

Each red box is 60px wide with word-break: keep-all. UAX #14 allows no break after the comma in 1,000,000 (LB25), after the full stops in 3.14 (LB25) or U.S.A. (LB29), or after the colon in 12:30 or after an opening parenthesis (LB14), and keep-all only removes break opportunities. So every box should break at its spaces only and let the long word overflow. The text of each box holds a Korean letter, so the text node is stored as UTF-16.
