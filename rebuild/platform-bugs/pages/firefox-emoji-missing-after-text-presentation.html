After U+1F600 U+FE0E is shaped once, plain U+1F600 draws as a missing-glyph box

Needs a fresh content process: open the page in a new window of a newly started browser, and don't reload. The page measures and draws U+1F600 (grinning face) in 32px Arial, then U+1F600 followed by U+FE0E (the text presentation selector), then plain U+1F600 again, and once more 3 seconds later. With ?via=dom the U+FE0E text is a DOM span instead of a canvas measurement. No text-style font has U+1F600 on macOS, so the second string may fall back either way, but plain U+1F600 should always be the 32px colour emoji.
