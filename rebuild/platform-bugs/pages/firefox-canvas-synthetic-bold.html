Synthetic bold widens glyphs by a different amount in canvas text and in DOM text on a HiDPI display

Needs devicePixelRatio 2. Ten U+2764 HEAVY BLACK HEART in bold Helvetica Neue. Helvetica Neue has no heart, and the fallback font that draws it has no bold face, so the glyphs get synthetic bold, which also widens each advance. The regular-weight row is the control. A canvas should measure what the DOM lays out.
